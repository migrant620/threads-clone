IMAGES
------------------------------------------------------------

All avatars and post images are original works produced for this prototype:

- Post media (p01..p04.webp) and avatars (a01..a05.webp): generated with an
  image-generation model (ImageGen) from original text prompts. They are NOT
  screenshots, recolourings, crops or derivatives of any photograph or
  illustration from the Threads application, and they carry no
  third-party copyright. The avatars are deliberately abstract so that no
  real person is depicted. They are distributed under the prototype's
  noncommercial study licence. Every image and its provenance is listed in
  this repository's asset notes.

No image obtained from the Threads application, and no image depicting an
identifiable person, is included in this distribution in any form.
